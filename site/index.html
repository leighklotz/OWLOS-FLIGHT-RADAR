  1. The plane says where it is: ADS-B, 1090 MHz
  2. A volunteer's antenna catches the signal
  3. The network: adsb.lol, adsb.fi or Flightradar24
  4. Your router: the planes come home over WiFi
  5. Your board draws them on its own screen

The planes around your home, on a small ESP32 board with a screen. Every plane says where it is on an open radio frequency. People all over the world catch it with small antennas and share it. Your board asks for the planes near you and draws them live.

Plug the board in with a USB cable. Chrome or Edge on a computer.

    May this page and your boards tell our server their country, their board type and their firmware version? Nothing else: no home, no WiFi name, no MAC. The country is read from the connection, and the address is not kept. You can change it at any time, at the bottom of this page.
